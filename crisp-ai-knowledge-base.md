## KNOWLEDGE BASE Q&A (verified facts only)

**What is Bodaiju Residences?**
A new Japanese-inspired serviced residence in Medini Zone C, Iskandar Puteri,
Johor. 802 units across two towers on 3.54 acres. Starting from RM299,000.
Expected completion 2028 (Tower A construction target 30 Nov 2027).

**Who is the developer?**
CI Medini Sdn Bhd, a wholly-owned subsidiary of Creed Group — a Japanese real
estate investor founded in 1996. Creed delivered the 928-unit Bodaiju Residences
in Phnom Penh (completed January 2020). The sales/marketing entity is Creed
Property Malaysia Sdn Bhd.

**Is this connected to Forest City or Country Garden?**
No. Creed Group is a privately-held Japanese company with no relationship to
Country Garden, Forest City, or any Chinese developer.

**Who is building it?**
MGB Construction & Engineering Sdn Bhd (a Bursa-listed builder, subsidiary of
LBS Bina Group), under a RM185.99 million contract disclosed on 12 Aug 2025.

**What unit types are available?**
- Type A: 1 bed, 1 bath, 463 sq ft — from RM299,000
- Type B: 2 bed, 2 bath, 753 sq ft — from RM432,000
- Type C: 3 bed, 2 bath, 893 sq ft — from RM512,000
- Type C1: 3 bed, 3 bath, 1,012 sq ft (dual-key) — from RM609,999
Prices are indicative starting prices, subject to unit, level and availability.

**What is the tenure?**
A 99-year leasehold serviced residence. Properties in Medini historically sit
on Private Lease Scheme (PLS) land, and IIB has offered PLS-to-freehold
conversion since Jan 2025. The exact title structure for this project is being
confirmed in writing — please consult an independent lawyer and review the SPA.
(Do not state freehold or PLS as definite.)

**Can foreigners buy? Is there a minimum price?**
Medini is exempt from Johor's usual RM1 million minimum price for foreign
buyers, so foreigners can buy from RM299,000 — rare in Malaysia. Eligibility is
still subject to current Johor State rules; confirm with a licensed lawyer.

**What does it cost a Singaporean / foreigner in total?**
Beyond the price: 8% stamp duty (from 1 Jan 2026), Johor state consent (~3% or
RM30,000 min), ~1.5% legal fees, 0.5% loan stamp duty — roughly 12% on top.
Try our cost calculator for a full MYR + SGD breakdown. Confirm with your
solicitor and bank.

**Where exactly is it?**
Medini Zone C, Iskandar Puteri. 9 km (~12–15 min) to the Tuas Second Link;
350 m to Sunway Big Box; ~1 km to Mall of Medini; ~2 km to LEGOLAND; 2–4 km to
EduCity international schools; Gleneagles Medini within the zone.

**When will it be completed?**
Expected 2028. Tower A has a construction completion target of 30 November 2027
(Bursa-disclosed). Tower B's start/completion dates are not yet confirmed.

**What is the RTS Link / JS-SEZ?**
The JB–Singapore RTS Link (~5-min crossing) targets service from January 2027;
the nearest station, Bukit Chagar, is ~25 km away (a drive, not walking
distance). Medini sits inside the Johor–Singapore Special Economic Zone
(signed 7 Jan 2025).

**What are the facilities?**
A facilities deck on Level 8 of Tower A — pool, gym, landscaped gardens and
more. The full verbatim facilities list is being finalised; our team can share
the official fact sheet.

**What is the maintenance fee / rental yield / Airbnb policy?**
Not yet confirmed. We'll share the official figures/policy when available rather
than estimate. Leave your contact and our team will follow up.

**Can I get financing? Can I use CPF?**
Malaysian banks (and the Malaysian arms of UOB/OCBC/StanChart) offer
cross-border mortgages, typically 60–70% loan-to-value for foreign buyers.
CPF cannot be used for overseas property. Subject to bank approval.

**How do I book a viewing / get the price list?**
Leave your name and WhatsApp/phone and our consultant will reach out, or visit
the sales gallery at Pusat Perdagangan Ion Akses, Persiaran Medini Sentral 6,
Iskandar Puteri.
